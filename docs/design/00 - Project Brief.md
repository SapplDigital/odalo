## Business objective

Explain Odalo’s services, demonstrate its creative personality, and convert interested visitors into audit requests or project enquiries.

The website should make three things immediately understandable:

1. What Odalo offers.
2. How the services work together.
3. How to start a conversation.

**Primary conversion:** Request a free audit.  
**Secondary conversion:** Explore services and plans.

## Brand personality

Odalo should feel creative, approachable, confident, clear about services and prices, warm, human, organized, capable, and international.

The existing navy, coral, cream, geometric typography, expressive italic typography, and mosaic logo form the foundation.

## Core concept

**Different pieces. One connected result.**

The mosaic communicates that websites, local visibility, video, and advertising work together.

It shapes the actual page composition: different panel sizes, shared edges, alternating surfaces, curved corners, and coordinated movement.

Whitespace remains essential. Headings and paragraphs need calm areas within the larger mosaic.

## Confirmed scope

- Worldwide positioning.
- Spanish, English, and German.
- Existing services and monthly plans retained as demo content.
- Services and prices editable later.
- Dynamic, responsive mosaic design.
- Planning and specifications only.

## Working assumptions

The four service families are inferred from the reference plans:

- Websites and maintenance.
- Local SEO and Google presence.
- Video and content.
- Paid advertising.

Their final names and scope can change without redesigning the site.

Prices, audit promises, tax wording, plan inclusions, and reporting claims are reference content. They require business confirmation before a commercial launch.
