# Odalo — Content, Plans, and Forms

## Voice

Write directly, using concrete descriptions and short sentences.

Prefer: “Una web clara, vídeos que explican y anuncios que llegan a las personas adecuadas.”

Avoid vague superlatives and unverified promises about growth or guaranteed results.

International positioning should appear naturally in the copy. Retain the warmth of the Spanish reference without limiting the audience geographically.

## Demo plans

Preserve the reference inclusions:

| Plan | Demo monthly price | Included |
|---|---:|---|
| Base | €59 | Hosting, maintenance, backups, 30 minutes of changes, basic Google business profile |
| Local | €290 | Base plus local SEO, review management, Google posts, monthly report |
| Captación | €590 | Local plus one advertising channel—Google or Meta—two reels monthly, results dashboard |
| 360 | €1,290 | Captación plus two advertising channels, one monthly recording session, twelve posts, SEO in two to three languages |

Captación receives the strongest visual emphasis.

Recommended badge wording: **“Plan destacado”**. The reference’s “best value” claim needs supporting business judgment before publication.

Each plan needs:

- Name.
- Price and billing interval.
- Summary.
- Inclusions.
- Exclusions.
- Plan-specific enquiry CTA.

Selecting a plan opens the audit page with that plan preselected.

Display **“Precios de ejemplo”** beside demo pricing.

The screenshot’s IGIC wording must be replaced with confirmed tax wording appropriate to the business and intended transactions before launch.

Advertising spend remains separate from agency fees if that reference condition is retained.

## Commercial details still to define

The references do not settle:

- Initial website design or setup fees.
- Minimum commitment and cancellation.
- Ownership of websites and creative assets.
- Revision limits.
- Recording-session location and travel.
- Advertising-account ownership.
- Exact reporting measures.
- Production timelines.
- Availability of multilingual deliverables by plan.

Keep these editable. Do not infer commercial commitments.

## Audit form

Required:

- Name.
- Email.
- Business name.
- Short description of the need.

Optional:

- Existing website.
- Country or time zone.
- Preferred language.
- Service interest.
- Plan interest.
- Phone number.

Website is optional so new businesses can enquire.

Use persistent labels, appropriate input types, helpful errors, and retained values after failure.

Provide an approved privacy notice and separate optional marketing consent if marketing is offered.

## Submission behavior

- Show progress while sending.
- Prevent duplicate submissions.
- Show success only after confirmed delivery.
- Preserve entries if delivery fails.
- Give a useful retry option and verified contact alternative.

Demo submissions must be explicitly identified as simulated if no delivery backend exists.

Confirmation wording must describe the real next step. Do not promise a response time until one is agreed.
