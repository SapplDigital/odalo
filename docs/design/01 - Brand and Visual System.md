# Odalo — Brand and Visual System

## Color palette

These are **provisional visual estimates** from the screenshots. Replace them with original brand values if available.

| Token | Proposed value | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Ink | `#203B57` | Headlines, body text, dark panels |
| Coral | `#D98165` | Primary actions, selected details, decorative pieces |
| Sand | `#EFE7D3` | Warm backgrounds and mosaic panels |
| Paper | `#F7F9F8` | Main background and light surfaces |
| Slate blue | `#50718B` | Supporting shapes and secondary panels |
| Rust | `#B65A40` | Occasional editorial accents; contrast must be checked |

Use Paper and Sand for most of the page. Navy establishes structure; Coral directs attention.

Suggested overall balance: 60% light surfaces, 25% navy, 10% coral, 5% slate blue.

Do not use white paragraph text on Coral without checking contrast. Navy text is the preferred starting combination.

## Typography

The references combine a heavy geometric sans serif with a narrow, expressive italic serif.

**Priority:** Preserve the original font families if the source files become available.

Provisional substitutes:

- [Outfit](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Outfit) for headings, navigation, body text, and controls.
- [Instrument Serif Italic](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Instrument+Serif) for expressive phrases.

These are proposed matches, not confirmed identifications.

| Role | Desktop | Mobile | Treatment |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Hero heading | 80–104 px | 44–56 px | Heavy sans, tight leading |
| Section heading | 48–64 px | 32–40 px | Bold sans with optional italic phrase |
| Tile heading | 28–36 px | 24–28 px | Semibold or bold |
| Body | 18–20 px | 17–18 px | Regular, 1.5–1.65 line height |
| Labels | 13–14 px | 13–14 px | Medium, restrained letter spacing |
| Buttons | 16–18 px | 16–18 px | Semibold |

Sizes should scale fluidly between these ranges.

Use italic serif for one meaningful phrase in a heading. Keep navigation, forms, prices, and long paragraphs in the sans serif.

Paragraph measure: approximately 50–65 characters per line.

## Logo

Preserve the existing symbol, lowercase wordmark, and terminal coral dot.

Required assets:

- Full-color horizontal SVG.
- Symbol-only SVG.
- Versions approved for light and dark backgrounds.
- Favicon derived from the symbol.
- Social sharing image.

Maintain clear space of at least half the symbol’s width around the full logo.

Decorative shapes may animate independently. The official logo should remain stable and recognizable.

## Shape vocabulary

Use shapes found in the logo:

- Squares and rectangles.
- Circles.
- Quarter-circles.
- Semicircles.
- Straight edges meeting rounded corners.

Text panels use mostly rectangular interiors. Circular and quarter-circle treatments belong primarily to decorative areas and imagery.

A signature panel can have one large curved corner and three square corners. Alternate its orientation across the mosaic.

Avoid giving every panel identical rounded corners.

## Spacing and surfaces

Spacing scale: **4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 96, 128 px**.

- Panel padding: 32–48 px desktop; 20–24 px mobile.
- Mosaic gutters: 12–16 px desktop; 8–12 px mobile.
- Section spacing: 96–128 px desktop; 56–72 px mobile.
- Content width: maximum 1440 px.
- Page margins: 48 px desktop, 32 px tablet, 20 px mobile.

Default surfaces are flat. Use restrained shadows only for floating navigation, dialogs, or other elements that genuinely sit above the page.

## Photography and illustration

Prefer real work, production scenes, business environments, and human details.

Crop photographs into the mosaic rather than placing them inside generic device mockups.

Use geometric illustration where photography is unavailable. Abstract pieces can suggest a browser, video frame, search result, or campaign.

Sample work must be labeled as sample work. Client names, testimonials, and results require verified sources.
