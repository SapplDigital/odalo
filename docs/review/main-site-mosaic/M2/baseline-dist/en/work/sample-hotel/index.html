Hospitality · Fictional hotel concept

Boutique hotel website concept

Self-initiated design for a fictional hotel, built as a local responsive prototype. It is not a client commission or a hotel launch, and there is no client or performance data.

Imagined brief

An imagined brief: a small, fictional harbour hotel with eight rooms needs a website that shows the rooms and the courtyard and makes it easy to ask for dates.

Proposed approach

Convey the place in a few seconds, present three room types clearly and give guests one obvious way to request dates.

Produced and proposed

Produced here: a local, responsive website prototype (home page, rooms, facilities and a date request form that sends nothing) with original illustrations. Proposed only, not produced: a local search profile and video content that would point to the same request.

  • Websites and maintenance
  • Local visibility
  • Video and content

Screens designed

The home page on desktop and on a phone with headline and date button, a presentation of three rooms, the courtyard and breakfast, and a date request form that sends nothing.

Image source. Original vector illustration and layout made for this concept with AI assistance. No photographs and no stock images.
