Local campaign concept for a physiotherapy practice

Self-initiated work for a fictional practice: a local prototype and two creative designs were produced here. There was no real client launch, and there is no client or performance data.

Imagined brief

An imagined brief: a small physiotherapy practice in a fictional neighbourhood depends on people nearby finding it and feeling comfortable getting in touch.

Proposed approach

Make the practice easy to understand and easy to reach: one page that explains the services in plain words, shows where the practice is and offers a single next step, with ads that lead to it.

Produced and proposed

Produced here: a local, responsive campaign destination page (a prototype that submits nothing), two original concept ad creatives (square and portrait) and a written local-presence plan. Proposed only, not produced: setting up a local business profile and running the advertising.

  • Local visibility
  • Paid advertising

Screens designed

The destination page on desktop and on a phone: an introduction, a "find us" section with address, access and hours, four service areas and a first-visit enquiry that submits nothing.

Concept ad creatives

Two concept creatives, square and portrait. They are designs only: not published, with no spend and no results.

Image source. Original vector illustrations, layouts and copy made for this concept with AI assistance. No photographs and no stock images. The practice, its address and its web address are fictional.

Proposed local-presence plan

Proposed work only. No live business profile, map listing, reviews or search results exist.

  1. One source of truth: the practice name, address, hours, access details and service list are written once and reused on the page, in the local business profile and in the ads.
  2. Matching descriptions: each service is described in the same plain words wherever it appears, so a search result, a profile and the page tell the same story.
  3. One enquiry destination: every ad and profile link points to the same page and the same first-visit enquiry, which at first asks only for an area and a preferred time.
  4. Delivery, proposed: the practice confirms who answers enquiries and how quickly; health details stay out of ads, forms and tracking.
