# Proposed local-presence plan: Tidewell Physiotherapy (fictional)

**Status: proposed work only.** No local business profile, map listing, reviews, ratings, rankings or search-result pages exist for this practice, and none are shown anywhere in this concept. The practice, its address (14 Millbrook Lane), hours and web address (`tidewell.example`) are fictional.

## What connects to what

1. **One record of local information.** The practice writes its name, address, opening hours, access details (step-free entrance, bus 12, walking time from the market) and its four service areas once. The destination page, the local business profile and the ads all copy from that single record, so a change is made in one place.
2. **The same plain service descriptions everywhere.** "Back and neck", "Knees, hips and ankles", "Shoulders and arms" and "Staying active" are described in the same words on the page, in the profile's service list and in ad copy. No claims about treatment results, no guarantees, no practitioner credentials or patient stories are used.
3. **One enquiry destination.** Both concept ads, the profile's website and appointment links and the page's buttons point to one page and one first-visit enquiry ("Ask about a first visit"). At first the enquiry asks only for an area and a preferred day and time; contact details are added by the practice's real system, and health details stay out of ads, forms and tracking.
4. **Delivery (not produced here).** To go live the practice would need to: confirm who answers enquiries and how quickly; claim and verify its own business profile; connect the enquiry to a real inbox or booking tool; and decide what, if anything, is measured and with what consent. None of that was done or simulated in this task.
